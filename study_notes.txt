Photosynthesis
Plants use chlorophyll to convert light energy into chemical energy. The equation is 6CO2 + 6H2O → C6H12O6 + 6O2. Light-dependent and light-independent reactions occur in chloroplasts.
Photosynthesis mainly happens in the roots of the plant.
The main product of photosynthesis is carbon dioxide.
Chlorophyll reflects green light and absorbs red and blue light.
Photosynthesis only occurs at night.

New Zealand Wars
The New Zealand Wars (1845-1872) were caused by land disputes and issues of sovereignty. The British Crown confiscated large areas of Māori land after the wars.
The New Zealand Wars started in 1900.
Māori won most of the major battles and kept all their land.
The wars were mainly about fishing rights.
The New Zealand Wars only happened in the South Island.

Solving Linear Equations
To solve an equation like 3x + 5 = 17, subtract 5 from both sides to get 3x = 12, then divide by 3. Always substitute your answer back to check.
When solving equations you should always multiply both sides by zero.
Linear equations can only have one variable called y.
The first step is always to add the numbers on both sides.
You never need to check your answer after solving an equation.

Cell Structure
Plant cells have a cell wall, chloroplasts and a large vacuole. Animal cells do not. Both have a nucleus, mitochondria, and cell membrane.
Animal cells have chloroplasts so they can make their own food.
The cell wall is found in both plant and animal cells.
Mitochondria are only found in plant cells.
The nucleus is not important for controlling the cell.
